Mentor Expectations

Clear guidelines for our PromathsBridge mentors

Your Role as a Mentor

As a PromathsBridge mentor, your primary responsibility is to guide first-year students through their transition to university life. You are not expected to be an academic tutor, but rather a supportive peer who can share experiences and provide guidance.

Regular Check-ins & Reports

Submit brief monthly reports about your mentorship sessions and check-ins with your mentee.

Transition Support Focus

Help first-years adapt to university life - campus navigation, time management, resource awareness, and social integration.

Academic Guidance (Optional)

While not required, you may offer study tips and course advice if you feel comfortable. Academic tutoring is NOT a prerequisite.

Guidance & Encouragement

Provide emotional support, share personal experiences, and encourage mentees to make the most of their university journey.

Key Points to Remember

What makes a great mentor

Mentorship Journey

A detailed look at the 12-week mentorship program

Program Timeline & Details

1

Weeks 1-4: Connection Phase

Focus: Building rapport and understanding the mentee's needs

  • Initial introduction and goal-setting sessions
  • Campus orientation and resource sharing
  • Mentor Responsibility: Submit initial connection report after first meeting
2

Weeks 5-8: Guidance Phase

Focus: Practical support for university transition

  • Time management and study habit discussions
  • Social integration and campus activity guidance
  • Important: Academic help is optional - focus on transition support
  • Mentor Responsibility: Submit bi-weekly progress reports
3

Weeks 9-12: Independence Phase

Focus: Fostering independence and closure

  • Encourage independent problem-solving
  • Review progress and celebrate achievements
  • Plan for continued self-guided growth
  • Mentor Responsibility: Submit final comprehensive report

Reporting Requirements

Simple, structured feedback system

Monthly Check-in Reports

Required

Brief reports documenting your monthly meetings with mentees (15-20 minutes to complete)

Progress Updates

Bi-weekly

Short updates on mentee's adaptation progress and any challenges faced

Final Program Report

End of Program

Comprehensive summary of the mentorship journey and recommendations

Remember: Your value is in your experience, not your academic expertise
